What's the best time to visit Himachal Pradesh? +

The best time depends on your preference! March to June is ideal for pleasant weather and sightseeing. October to February is perfect for snow lovers, especially in Manali and Shimla. July–September is monsoon season — beautiful but roads can be tricky. Spiti Valley is best visited June to September as roads close in winter.

What's included in the travel packages? +

All our packages include hotel accommodation, meals (as specified), local transport, sightseeing, and a dedicated guide. Premium packages add travel insurance, adventure activities, spa sessions, and airport transfers. Check individual package details for the full breakdown.

Is it safe for solo travellers and women? +

Absolutely! Himachal Pradesh is one of India's safest states for travellers. Our guides are trained, vetted, and local. We offer dedicated women-only group trips and 24/7 emergency support. Solo female travellers make up 30% of our bookings!

What's your cancellation and refund policy? +

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the trip start date for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours receive a 50% refund or the option to reschedule at no extra cost. Natural disaster cancellations are fully refunded. We're flexible!

Do I need any permits for Spiti Valley or Rohtang? +

Yes — Rohtang Pass requires an e-permit (₹550/person, limited daily). Inner Line Permit (ILP) is needed for areas beyond Kaza in Spiti. Don't worry — we handle all permit arrangements for you as part of the package. Just bring your ID!

How fit do I need to be for trekking? +

It depends on the trek! Easy treks like Triund or Bijli Mahadev require basic fitness — if you can walk 6-8 km comfortably, you're good. Moderate treks need stamina training 2-3 weeks prior. We always assess fitness before difficult treks and provide acclimatization days at altitude.

Essential Himalayan Travel Protocols

  • Ascending vehicles have right of way: On narrow mountain roads, always give way to vehicles climbing uphill.
  • Engine braking on descents: Downshift to 1st or 2nd gear on steep downhills to avoid brake fade.
  • Acclimatization rule: When ascending above 3,000m (e.g. Spiti Valley or Rohtang Pass), stay relaxed on Day 1.
  • Mobile network tips: Jio and BSNL provide the most dependable connectivity in remote trans-Himalayan valleys.
